# Cindermaw: direction

## The world

A mining moon cracked open over something that was already awake. The 9th Breach Company holds what is left of
the refinery decks; everything below them belongs to the Maw, and both sides have learned to chain the other's
monsters and machines to their own colours.

Reference points, in our own words:

- **Materials**: riveted iron plate, hazard stripes, rusted pipe; raw flesh walls, bone, slag, lava, toxic slime.
- **Era**: a 1993 PC shooter on a 320 x 200 screen. Digitised-looking sprites with no outlines, a short harsh
  palette, ordered dithering, a grey status bar bolted across the bottom of the screen.
- **Mood**: loud, grimy, fast. Heavy metal through an FM sound card. Nothing is cute.

## The two sides

| | name | colour | where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| player | **the 9th**, Breach Company | toxic green `#8cf02a` (dark `#2c6a10`, light `#e4ff9a`) | visors, shoulder plates, tanks, wings, eye-glow, banners, health LEDs, deploy zone |
| rival | **the Maw** | hot blood red `#e0240c` (dark `#6a0c06`, light `#ffb060`) | the same surfaces |

Bright acid green against deep hot red: they separate in lightness as well as hue. Each of the 31 roles is one
body that both sides field (marines and machines, or bound hellspawn), so team identity is carried by large glowing
surfaces, never by the body type: the ground itself stays rust, iron, ash and dull flesh, and no scenery uses pure
team green or team red at full brightness.

Type: **Jersey 10** (SIL OFL) for every heading, number and button: a tall chunky pixel face; **Silkscreen** (SIL
OFL) for small labels. All capitals. Numbers in the status bar are red LED numerals in black wells.

## What the interface is made of

Grey cast metal with one-pixel bevels (light top-left, dark bottom-right), square corners, rivets, hazard tape for
warnings, black recessed wells holding red numerals. No rounded corners, no gradients, no gloss.

- **Battle**: one status bar bolted across the bottom of the screen, like the shooters of the time: HEALTH, SCRAP
  and SQUAD in big LED numerals either side of **the Warden**, a helmeted skull emblem in a porthole whose visor
  goes from green to amber to red and cracks as the keep's health falls. The rival is a thin strip at the top: a
  boss health bar. The shop is a rack of weapon slots under the bar with the cost on an LED tag.
- **Home**: a title screen. The logo in bevelled iron over the key art, then a vertical text menu with a skull
  cursor (NEW RAID, ARSENAL, HUNTS, ARMOURY...) instead of the base game's stacked cards, with the player's
  stats as a status bar along the bottom and the tab bar as numbered weapon keys 1 to 5.
- Everything else: restyled from the same parts.

The battle canvas is drawn at a third of the screen's resolution and crushed to 34 colours with ordered dither.

## The cast

plus one glowing team surface. Marines and machines carry guns; hellspawn carry claws, bone and fire.
