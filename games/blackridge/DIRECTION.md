# Blackridge: direction

## The world

Blackridge is a cold mountain corridor of airstrips, rail yards, a dam and a refinery, and two invented forces are
fighting for it one grid square at a time. The player watches from a drone feed: a real-looking battlefield, small
soldiers with real proportions, armour, smoke, and a command display laid over it.

Reference points, in our own words:

- **Materials:** poured concrete, corrugated plate, sandbags, scorched brick, wet asphalt, olive nylon and gunmetal.
  Nothing is outlined; everything is shaded smoothly and a little dirty.
- **Era:** the near present. Plate carriers, helmets with rails, drones, light armour. No real armies, flags,
  insignia, people or places.
- **Mood:** overcast, tense, quiet between bursts. Desaturated and cold; the only saturated things on screen are
  the two sides' colours and fire.

## The two sides

| | colour | where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player: **Task Force Halcyon** | cold cyan `#2ec4e6` (dark `#0f6f8c`, light `#a8ecff`) | helmet band, armband, shield and vehicle panels, the ring under every unit, health bars, deploy zone |
| Rival: the **Meridian Pact** | signal orange `#f26a1b` (dark `#9c3a08`, light `#ffc089`) | the same places |

Opposite in hue (blue against orange survives every common colour-blindness and the desaturating lens), and the
orange sits darker than the cyan. Uniforms are neutral on both sides; a soldier's side is the glowing ring at his
feet first, then his band and panel.

## Type

- Display: a condensed grotesque, system only: `"DIN Condensed", "Avenir Next Condensed", "Roboto Condensed",
  "Arial Narrow", sans-serif`, upper case, widely tracked.
- Text and every number: a monospace stack: `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, "Roboto Mono", monospace`, light
  weights, small sizes, labels in upper case with a leading code (`OP-07`, `GRID 4C`).

## What the interface is made of

A tactical command display: dark glass over the feed, hairline rules, square corners with one chamfered corner or
corner brackets, map-grid lines and coordinates in the background, mil-spec callouts (`// SECTOR 03`), thin type.
No wood, no parchment, no rounded cartoon buttons, no shiny coins: currencies are flat stencil glyphs (a supply
chit, a fuel cell). Buttons are flat plates with a hairline border that fill on press; the primary action is a wide
amber-edged "DEPLOY" bar. Health bars are thin segmented strips. The home screen is an operations brief: a status
header strip, the key art as a surveillance frame with brackets and coordinates, the current operation as a brief
card, and a left-aligned list of orders rather than a grid of cards. The battle HUD is a thin top telemetry strip
and a flat unit rack along the bottom.
