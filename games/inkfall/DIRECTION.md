# Inkfall: direction

## The world

A hand scroll of ink-wash painting that has come to life: two clans, brushed in black ink on warm rice paper, fight
across misty mountains, pine ridges, bamboo groves and temple yards. Everything is a brush mark, and most of the
picture is empty paper.

Reference points, in our own words:

- **Materials:** soot ink on unsized paper. Wet washes that bleed at the edge, dry-brush strokes that break up,
  a carved red seal pressed on top.
- **Era:** an imagined age of clan wars; straw hats, lacquered armour, shrines and paper lanterns. No real clans,
  crests, people or places.
- **Mood:** a sudden hush. Quiet, pale and nearly monochrome; the only saturated things on screen are the two
  sides' colours.

## The two sides

| | colour | where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player: the **Indigo** clan | deep indigo wash `#1f5878` (dark `#123a52`, light `#8fd3d6`) | sashes, banners, hats, shields, keep roofs, health bars, deploy zone |
| Rival: the **Vermilion** clan | seal red `#e2482b` (dark `#9a2716`, light `#ffb690`) | the same places |

Indigo is dark and cool, vermilion is bright and warm: about 20 points of lightness apart as well as opposite in
hue, so they separate for colour-blind players. Each unit carries its side's colour flat (unshaded) on one big area.

## Type

- Display: **Yuji Syuku** (SIL OFL, bundled in `public/fonts/` with its licence): a brush-written serif face, used
  for headings, numbers, buttons and the lettering on the battle canvas, widely letter-spaced; a few headings run
  vertically.
- Text: a system serif stack (`"Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, "Songti SC", Georgia, serif`).

## What the interface is made of

Paper and ink. Panels are off-white sheets with a brushed or torn edge and no rounded plastic corners; rules are
dry-brush strokes; buttons are a single brush stroke of ink with paper-coloured lettering (secondary buttons are an
inked outline); the primary action, costs, badges and notifications are red seal stamps (square, slightly rough,
pale carved lettering). Health bars are thin ink lines with the side's colour laid along them. The tab bar is a
strip of paper with ink icons and a seal dot on the active tab. The home screen is a hanging scroll: the title runs
down the right edge in a vertical column, the key art is the painting, and the Battle button is a large round seal.

## The battlefield

Paper ground with pale washes for paths and plazas, kerbs as brush lines, and scenery kept to the edges: misty
mountain ranges, pines, bamboo, torii gates, stone lanterns, koi ponds, plum blossom. Effects are ink: splats, brush
slashes, enso rings, falling petals; nothing glows. Arena buildings are plaster-and-timber walls with tiled copings,
thatched houses, pagoda towers, bamboo fences, straw bales and koi ponds.
